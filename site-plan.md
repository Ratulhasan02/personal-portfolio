# Personal Portfolio — Site Plan

## Purpose
The purpose of this website is to showcase my skills, projects, education, and development journey as a Computer Science and Engineering student. It helps recruiters, internship providers, and potential clients learn about my abilities and explore my work.

## Target Visitors

- Recruiters and internship providers
- Potential clients
- Developers and fellow students

## Pages and Sections
This is a single-page portfolio website with the following sections:

1. Home — Introduction and navigation
2. About — Personal and academic background
3. Skills — Technical skills and current learning interests
4. Projects — Project cards, technology filters, and project links
5. Education — Academic information
6. Learning & Development — Current learning goals
7. Services — Development services
8. Contact — Contact form and professional links

The fixed sidebar links to Home, About, Skills, Projects, Education, Learning, and Contact. Services is present as a page section but is not a sidebar navigation item.

## Good User Experience
The website should be responsive, easy to navigate, readable on mobile and desktop, visually consistent, and provide clear access to projects and contact information.
